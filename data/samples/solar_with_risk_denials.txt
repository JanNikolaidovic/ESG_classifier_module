The project will install 5 MW of solar panels on the roof of the sponsor's existing distribution centre. Total cost is 4.2 million EUR, financed 30% by equity and 70% by a seven-year loan.

No land acquisition or resettlement is required, as all works take place on an existing building. A biodiversity screening found no protected species or sensitive habitats at or near the site. The installation does not use or discharge any water, and no hazardous materials will be stored on site.

The installer has recorded no fatalities or serious injuries in the last ten years. Suppliers have been screened, and no forced or child labour has been identified in the panel supply chain.

The company has never been investigated for corruption or bribery, and no material litigation is pending against it.
