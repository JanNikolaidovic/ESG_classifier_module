The SunGrid Logistics Rooftop Programme will install 18 MW of solar photovoltaic capacity on the roofs of 22 existing warehouses owned by the sponsor across three industrial parks. The electricity will be consumed on site to power refrigeration, lighting and electric forklift charging, with any surplus exported to the grid under a net metering arrangement.

Total project cost is 14.5 million EUR. The sponsor will contribute 25% as equity, and the remaining 75% is requested as a ten-year amortising term loan. Annual electricity savings are projected at 2.1 million EUR, giving a payback period of approximately seven years. The sponsor has operated the warehouse portfolio for twelve years and reported revenue of 86 million EUR and EBITDA of 15 million EUR in the last financial year.

All installations will be on existing buildings, so no new land is required. Structural surveys of each roof have been completed by an independent engineering firm, and reinforcement works are needed on two buildings. Panels and inverters will be procured from established tier-one manufacturers through a competitive tender, and installation will be carried out by an experienced contractor during normal working hours over a period of nine months.

The installations are expected to cover about 40% of the warehouses' annual electricity demand and reduce the company's energy costs. The sponsor has an internal facilities team that will be trained to carry out routine inspections and cleaning, and a five-year operations and maintenance contract will be signed with the installer. Performance will be monitored remotely through an online platform, and monthly production reports will be shared with the lender.

The company is privately owned and has an independent auditor. Financial statements for the last three years are available, and no material litigation is pending.
